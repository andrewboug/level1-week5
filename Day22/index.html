Here's how to play guitar!

Using your fretting hand

If you are right-handed, you are going to want to use your left hand to fret the strings. Place your fingers on the frets in a way that allows you to press down firmly without muting adjacent strings.

Strumming with your opposite hand

You are going to want to use your right hand to strum the strings. Using a pick, which is a triangular piece of plastic or metal, hold the pick between your thumb and index finger, and use a smooth, controlled motion to strum the strings.
